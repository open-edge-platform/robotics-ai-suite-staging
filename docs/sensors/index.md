# Sensors

## Introduction

This section describes how to connect sensors to the development kit and stream their data. It covers physical wiring, driver bring-up, and hardware time synchronization.

- **[Cameras](cameras/index.md)**: Connect and stream uncompressed video and volumetric depth for computer vision and manipulation.
- **[LiDAR](lidar/index.md)**: Bring up 3D point clouds for mapping and obstacle avoidance.
- **[IMU](imu/index.md)**: Process high-frequency motion data for state estimation.
