# Vision AI

## Introduction

Vision AI frameworks cover the full perception workflow: training and fine-tuning vision
models, optimizing them for the edge, and running them as real-time inference pipelines. A
robot uses these models to detect and locate objects, segment free space and obstacles for
navigation, and inspect parts for defects.

The frameworks target OpenVINO, so perception runs across the Intel CPU, GPU, and NPU and
feeds results into the ROS 2 pipelines used by the [blueprints](../../reference-implementations/index.md).
